Trivia Lens — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

What Trivia Lens does

Trivia Lens is a game-show trivia game. You spin a category wheel, play solo or pass-and-play party rounds, take a daily challenge, earn badges, keep streaks, and send challenge links to friends. The game runs entirely on your device.

Data we collect

  • None. Trivia Lens has no accounts, no sign-up, and no servers that receive your data. The game makes no network requests to us and includes no analytics or advertising trackers.
  • On-device storage: your settings, stage name, personal records, badges, streaks, and game history live in your browser's local storage on your device only. We never receive them.
  • Challenge links: when you create a challenge link, your stage name, the category and level, and your score are encoded into the link itself, on your device. The link only goes where you send it — it is never transmitted to us, and the game reads it locally on the device that opens it.
  • Lensers preview: the optional Lensers presence preview shows sample data inside the app. Turning it on sends nothing anywhere; every other player shown is sample data, and nothing leaves your device.

Data we don't collect

We don't collect names, emails, locations, or device identifiers. We don't sell personal data, run advertising trackers, or share data with data brokers — there is no collected data to sell or share.

Deleting your data

Your game data is yours and stays on your device. You can erase it at any time using the reset option in the app's Settings, or by clearing your browser's site data for Trivia Lens.

Children

Trivia Lens is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone — the game collects no data at all.

Changes

We'll update this page if the policy changes. Continued use after changes means you accept the update.
